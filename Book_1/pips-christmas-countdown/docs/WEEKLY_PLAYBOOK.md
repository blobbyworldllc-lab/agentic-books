# Blobby World Book Factory: Catalog Strategy and Weekly Playbook

Owner: Vijay (Bizatree LLC, pen name Blobby World Official). Written 2026-11-01. Read PROJECT.md first for the Pip's Christmas Countdown build details.

## 1. The problem

Pip's Christmas Countdown is seasonal: it sells hard from mid-November to about Dec 18, then falls to near zero until next October. One seasonal title cannot carry income. The fix is a catalog: many titles in one recognizable universe, a mix of evergreen books (sell all year) and seasonal books (each one spikes every year), all pointing readers to each other.

## 2. What the research says (Nov 2026)

- Evergreen early-learning coloring for ages 2-6 (alphabet, numbers, first words, vehicles, farm animals) sells year round. Parents rebuy and are not price sensitive for quality.
- Kids 4-8 is the volume heart of the market but crowded. Winning angle: a fun twist (dinosaurs doing everyday things, monster trucks at the beach).
- Bold and easy, large-print books for seniors and beginners have low competition and sell at adult prices ($10.99-$14.99, about $4-5 royalty).
- Seasonal kids books (Christmas, Easter, Halloween, Mother's Day, summer, back-to-school) work if published 6-8 weeks early and relisted yearly. A seasonal catalog compounds.
- A recurring character plus KDP Series linking turns one-off buyers into series buyers.
- Single-sided pages with blank backs avoid the top one-star complaint (marker bleed).
- Kids pricing sweet spot: $9.99 (crosses the better royalty band). Adult: $10.99-$14.99.
- Sources: inkfluenceai.com/blog/coloring-book-niches-2026, kdpbuilder.com/blog/best-coloring-book-niches. Re-verify with live Amazon data every Friday; these blogs are directional only.

## 3. The catalog model

Three lanes, all under the Pip and Friends universe for kids (Blobby World Official):

1. Evergreen core (publish first, sells every month): Pip's ABC, Pip's Numbers, Pip's First Words, Pip's Farm Friends, Pip's Vehicles, Pip's Dino Day.
2. Seasonal ladder (each earns every year): Valentine's, Easter, Mother's Day, Summer, Back-to-School, Halloween, Thanksgiving, Christmas.
3. Bundles and upgrades: once 3+ books exist, publish "Pip's Big Book of ..." compilations at $14.99-$16.99 (higher royalty, same art).

Later, separate lane under a different pen name (never Blobby World): bold-and-easy large-print adult/senior books at $12.99. Test only after 5+ kids titles are live.

## 4. Weekly cadence

Friday 6pm ET (automatic scheduled run starts the cycle):
- Research 30-45 min: Amazon search/bestseller pages for 5-8 candidate niches; check BSR and review counts of top results, cover quality, keyword autocomplete; check upcoming seasonal deadlines in section 3.
- Score each candidate (section 5) and pick ONE.
- Produce the launch kit so Saturday starts at full speed: idea brief, all captions, per-page Gemini prompts, KDP listing draft (title, subtitle, description, 7 keywords, 3 categories), cover brief, marketing kit outline.
- Save to the repo under books/<slug>/ and send a push notification with the pick and why.

Saturday: generate art in Gemini through Chrome (laptop on, signed in, both Google accounts; roughly 45 images per account per day, so 30-40 page toddler books fit in one day, 50 page books take two).
Sunday: capture, clean, build interior and cover, QA (fonts embedded, 300 ppi, view pages).
Monday: upload to KDP, run Previewer, publish (needs Vijay's go-ahead before the final publish click).
Tuesday-Thursday: social graphics, free printable pack, Pinterest pins, Author Central and A+ update, add the book to every other book's back matter list.
Next Friday: results review, then the next pick.

Realistic throughput: one new 30-40 page book per week, or one 50 page book every 8-10 days. Keep two books ahead of the seasonal deadline at all times.

## 5. Niche scoring (0-5 each, pick the highest total)

- Demand: top-10 results have BSR under 100,000 in Books; several under 30,000 is strong.
- Gap: top results have under 200 reviews, weak covers, generic titles, or no series.
- Fit: reuses Pip, Little Star and the existing art style.
- Timing: evergreen, or seasonal with at least 6 weeks to the deadline.
- Speed: can finish inside one week.
- Hook: one sentence that makes it different from the top 10.
Reject anything using famous characters, brands, or trademarks.

## 6. Sales flywheel (all free)

- Series: link every Pip book in one KDP Series; Amazon shows it on every listing.
- Back matter in every book lists the other titles.
- Author Central: one page, all books, bio, photos.
- A+ Content for each book (free), built from page art.
- Free printable pack per book is the lead magnet for Pinterest (5-10 pins a week, pins keep earning for months).
- Short "color with Pip" videos reuse the existing video pipeline.
- Reviews: ask real buyers only. Never trade anything for reviews. Insert a polite review-request line on the last page.
- Bundles at $14.99+ once 3 books are live.

## 7. Decision rules (check every Friday in KDP reports)

- A title selling 3+ units/week in its first 4 weeks: make a sequel in that niche within 2 weeks.
- A title under 1 unit/week after 6 weeks: change cover or title/subtitle/keywords once, wait 3 weeks, then stop that niche.
- Seasonal books: relist/refresh keywords every year 8 weeks before the date.
- Spend: stay at $0 ads until one title proves 3+ units/week organically; then test $3-5/day Sponsored Products on the best title only.

## 8. Risks

- Amazon scrutinizes bulk AI content: keep quality high, keep AI disclosure accurate on every book, do not clone other publishers' books.
- Gemini daily image limits; keep the second Google account signed in.
- The first book has zero reviews; early ranking depends on free traffic.
- Interior has not been proof-printed; order one author copy before scaling.
- Never reuse copyrighted characters; keep all art original.
